# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

These projects should be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, or background.

Behavior we expect:

- be respectful and considerate
- accept constructive feedback gracefully
- focus on what is best for the project and its users

Behavior we do not accept:

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- discriminatory or sexualized language or imagery
- publishing other people's private information without permission
- sustained disruption of discussions or contributions

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and discussions, and when representing a project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to ops@pyxis3.ai. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, remove contributions, or ban anyone who violates this code.
